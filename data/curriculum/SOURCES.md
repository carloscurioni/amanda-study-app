## Notes
- The Year 10 / Year 11 split follows the syllabus order: roughly the first half of the course in Y10 and the rest plus revision in Y11. Y11 uses weeks 28–33 for topic-by-topic review and weeks 34–37 for the external exams, as FORMAT.md says.
- History: Cambridge lets each school choose its options. These files use the most common route: Core Option B (20th century) and Depth Study B (Germany, 1918–45), with Paper 4 (Alternative to Coursework). The Gulf key question is not in the 2027–2028 syllabus, so it is left out.
- Geography: these files use the new 2027–2029 syllabus (rivers, coasts, ecosystems, tectonics, climate change, populations, towns and cities, development, economies, resources). It applies to both the current Y10 and the current Y11 cohort.
- Business: 0264 replaces 0450 from the 2027 exams. Cambridge says the content has not changed much, so the topics follow the 0450 section structure (1–6).
- Psychology: Cambridge IGCSE 0266 exists, so no GCSE specification was needed.
- Spanish/French: topic names and objectives are written in English, with keywords in the target language. Grammar points come from the syllabus grammar lists. The Brazil comparisons and Francophone/Hispanic culture examples are teaching choices, not syllabus requirements.

## Notes on pacing decisions
- DP1 (Y12) covers roughly the first half of each course plus IA planning in weeks 34–35; DP2 (Y13) finishes content in term 1, then synthesis/exam practice in term 2, topic revision in weeks 28–33 and the May exam session in weeks 34–37 (per FORMAT.md).
- HL files follow the same calendar as SL. Where SL has a practical/data-skills or "in context" consolidation week, HL uses that slot for an HL-only topic; shared topics carry an extra "HL:" objective for the additional higher level content.
- Sciences: Collaborative sciences project placed in DP1; IA (scientific investigation) planned end of DP1, carried out and written up in DP2 term 1.
- Computer Science follows the new guide (first assessment 2027), since students starting DP1 in 2026 are assessed under it; A4.2, A4.3, A1.4, A3.4, B3.2 and B4 treated as HL-only, following the RevisionDojo subtopic list. The annual pre-released case study is taught in DP2.
- TOK: optional themes chosen are Knowledge and technology and Knowledge and language (schools choose two of five). Exhibition completed in DP1 term 3; essay written in DP2 term 1, submitted early term 2. TOK has no written exam, so the review/exam weeks required by the calendar are framed as TOK reflection on subject exams.

## Decisões de planejamento
- History: o Y12 (DP1 em 2026–27, provas em 2028) segue o novo guia. Focused study "Independence and identity" (Haitian Revolution; Kenyan independence), thematic study "Authoritarian rule" (Stalin, Vargas, Mao, regime militar brasileiro). No HL, regional study das Américas: "Political developments in Latin America (1934–2020)", e começa "The Americas during the Cold War". O Y13 (provas em maio de 2027) segue o guia de 2020: Paper 1 "Rights and protest", Paper 2 "Authoritarian states" e "The Cold War", HL History of the Americas.
- Psychology: Y12 e Y13 seguem o guia de 2027 (as duas turmas começaram em 2025 ou depois).
- Geography: SL faz as opções G (Urban environments) e D (Geophysical hazards); o HL soma a opção A (Freshwater) e a HL core extension.
