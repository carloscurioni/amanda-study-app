## Notes
- The Year 10 / Year 11 split follows the syllabus order: roughly the first half of the course in Y10 and the rest plus revision in Y11. Y11 uses weeks 28–33 for topic-by-topic review and weeks 34–37 for the external exams, as FORMAT.md says.
- History: Cambridge lets each school choose its options. These files use the most common route: Core Option B (20th century) and Depth Study B (Germany, 1918–45), with Paper 4 (Alternative to Coursework). The Gulf key question is not in the 2027–2028 syllabus, so it is left out.
- Geography: these files use the new 2027–2029 syllabus (rivers, coasts, ecosystems, tectonics, climate change, populations, towns and cities, development, economies, resources). It applies to both the current Y10 and the current Y11 cohort.
- Business: 0264 replaces 0450 from the 2027 exams. Cambridge says the content has not changed much, so the topics follow the 0450 section structure (1–6).
- Psychology: Cambridge IGCSE 0266 exists, so no GCSE specification was needed.
- Spanish/French: topic names and objectives are written in English, with keywords in the target language. Grammar points come from the syllabus grammar lists. The Brazil comparisons and Francophone/Hispanic culture examples are teaching choices, not syllabus requirements.
