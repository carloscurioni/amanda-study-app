# Fontes — Primary Y3 e Y4

Arquivos: `data/curriculum/primary/Y3-*.json` e `Y4-*.json` (pt, pt_ort, pt_leit, bss, en, en_read, en_spell, en_guided, ipc, math).

## English, Maths (en, en_read, en_spell, en_guided, math)
- National curriculum in England: primary curriculum (statutory guidance, DfE 2013) — https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-primary-curriculum
- English programmes of study: key stages 1 and 2, incl. Appendix 1 (spelling, Years 3–4 and statutory word list) and Appendix 2 (vocabulary, grammar and punctuation) — https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7de93840f0b62305b7f8ee/PRIMARY_national_curriculum_-_English_220714.pdf
- Primary national curriculum (full document, incl. Mathematics Year 3 and Year 4 programmes of study) — https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/18300/1/PRIMARY_national_curriculum.pdf
- en_guided follows the VIPERS reading domains (Vocabulary, Infer, Predict, Explain, Retrieve, Summarise), which map to the KS2 reading content domains.

## IPC (ipc)
- IPC Milepost 2 unit overview, Years 3–4 (Stewart Fleming Primary School) — https://stewartfleming.bromley.sch.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/curriculumipcunits-milepost2-year34.pdf
- What is the IPC? (Twinkl Teaching Wiki) — https://www.twinkl.com/teaching-wiki/ipc
- Milepost 2: Years 3 and 4 (The British School Caracas) — https://tbscaracas.com/milepost-2-years-3-and-4/

Units used: Y3 — Brainwave, Footprints from the Past, Active Planet, Flow, Saving the World, Gateways to the World, Buildings. Y4 — Brainwave, Temples, Tombs and Treasures, How Humans Work, Explorers and Adventurers, They Made a Difference, Chocolate, Different Places, Similar Lives.
