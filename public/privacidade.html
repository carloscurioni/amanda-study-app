🦉 Privacy Notice

Study with Lumi · version 1 · October 2026

Study with Lumi is a study app for children, used only by invited families. This notice explains, in plain language, what data we keep, why, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Who registers the children

Only a parent or legal guardian creates the family account and the children’s profiles, authorising the use of the data for the purposes below (Brazilian General Data Protection Law – LGPD, art. 14). Children do not use e-mail: they open their own profile with a PIN, on a device where a parent has already logged in.

What data we keep

  • Parents: name, e-mail, password (stored only as a cryptographic code, never as text) and the date of authorisation.
  • Children: first name or nickname, school year, chosen character and PIN (also stored as a cryptographic code).
  • Activities: the week’s topics, questions, answers, marking, coins, shop items and vouchers.
  • Usage: the number of requests made to the artificial intelligence and the estimated cost per day.

We do not ask for addresses, schools, phone numbers, documents or photos of faces. Please ask children not to write this information in their answers.

Artificial intelligence

Lumi is an artificial intelligence, not a person. To create, explain and mark exercises, the app sends Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) only what each request needs: the week’s topics, the questions, the answers and, when the child marks a sheet with the camera, the photo of the sheet. Photos are not stored by the app. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train models by default. The AI can make mistakes, so parents can review the marking and use the report button when something looks wrong.

Messages sent with the 💬 button

Messages sent with the 💬 button (question, suggestion or problem) go to the app administrator. Along with the text, the app sends the family name, who wrote it (the parent or the child profile that was open), the screen they were on, the app version and the browser type, to help understand the problem. Messages are stored on the app’s server and may be sent by e-mail to the support address (through the Resend service). Please ask children not to write personal information in these messages.

Where the data is kept

On Cloudflare servers. Each child’s data is kept in a separate space, and each family can only access its own data. Access is protected by a secure session and a password.

Your rights

  • View and download: Parents’ area → Family → “Download all family data”.
  • Correct: edit names and school year in Parents’ area → Family.
  • Delete: delete a child’s profile or the whole family account in Parents’ area → Family. Deletion is permanent.
  • Questions: use the 💬 button in the corner of the screen or contact the administrator who sent your invitation.

This notice may be updated; important changes will be communicated to parents.
